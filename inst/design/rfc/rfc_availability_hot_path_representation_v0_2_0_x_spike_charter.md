## One question

> Can one bounded columnar/chunked diagnostic writer replace the current
> list-of-one-row-data-frames path while preserving exact direct-run,
> interrupted/resumed, persisted, reopened, and explained evidence on the
> shared availability fold?

## Cheaper prerequisite

Question: which lane dominates wall time and allocation pressure in the current
availability-aware flat fold on a shortened, public, production-shaped
synthetic fixture? Answer: `DIAGNOSTICS_DOMINANT` at the registered 40-pulse
shape (`dev/spikes/availability-hot-path-representation/lane_profile_findings.md`):
diagnostics 67.0% of in-loop samples, provider 31.2%, valuation 1.0%, residual
0.8%; final `rbind` 41.9% and row construction 24.7% individually. That answer
authorizes comparing the diagnostic representation first. It does not prove
dominance at the external 757-pulse shape, and no result of this spike may be
extrapolated across pulse counts.

## Smallest runnable fork

One seam: replace diagnostic-row accumulation and final binding behind the
existing fold and output-handler boundary: the per-row `data.frame()`
constructor (`R/availability-economics.R:386-432`), the retained list and
`append_diagnostic()` (`R/fold-engine.R:318-327`) with its append sites
(`611-654` and the others), the final `do.call(rbind, diagnostic_rows)` and
single persistence call (`R/fold-engine.R:1082-1108`), and the handler's
write-time `MAX(diagnostic_seq) + 1` renumbering (`R/backtest-runner.R:426-446`).

Unchanged: provider resolution, valuation, strategy invocation, accounting and
ledger events, the `run_diagnostics` schema and every stored column, public
diagnostics as data frames/tibbles, `ledgr_run_explain()`, and the transaction
owner (`output_handler$run_transaction()` around the whole loop at
`R/fold-engine.R:1116`; exception evidence only after rollback at 1120-1140).
Vectorized base assignment and `collapse::setv()` are permitted details inside
the one alternative; buffer ownership and chunk policy are the fork's choice,
not the charter's. The executor begins with the smallest runnable fork; its
failures generate at most ten initial cases. No expected answers, witness rows,
or checker exist before the fork has failed.

## Comparison arms

1. The current production list-of-one-row-data-frames implementation.
2. One bounded columnar/chunked diagnostic-writer implementation.

No third arm. Both arms run the lane profile's public fixture family
(563 instruments, 505-member axis, flat, zero holdings), regenerated by the runner.

## Evidence claims

Contract-level categories for later fork-derived cases; not a witness list.

1. Identical diagnostic schema, values, row count, nullable meaning, and the
   authoritative pulse/stage/axis order (`v0_2_0_0_spec.md:424-429`;
   `contracts.md:462-471`; reader at `R/availability-results.R:24-29`).
2. Continuous, exactly-once `diagnostic_seq` across direct execution,
   deliberate `max_pulses` interruption, and resume, with no second write
   through `write_run_evidence()` (`R/backtest-runner.R:441-446`).
3. Identical completion status, ledger events, fills, equity, and strategy
   state, compared through ledgr's own run and snapshot identity.
4. Identical fresh-connection diagnostics and `ledgr_run_explain()` results
   (`contracts.md:877-884`), including exception evidence after a forced
   fold rollback.
5. Same-host wall and process peak-memory measurements with explicit timing
   boundaries and no public performance claim.

Existing append, resume, rollback (`test-availability-economics.R`) and parity
tests remain the regression net; the fork adds cases only where it fails.

## One kill and recharter condition

If the alternative removes diagnostic construction and final binding as the
largest measured lane, but the unchanged production-shaped run still breaches
its registered resource envelope, close the spike without adding another
alternative and route provider resolution into a separate RFC or spike
question. The envelope is registered once, before the first fork run, on the
public fixture at 757 pulses: `t_loop` at most 1,800 seconds (the external
control's frozen ceiling) and process peak working set at most 4,096 MiB,
measured as below. The current path is not obliged to finish that shape.

## Executor deliverables

Exactly the three items of `spike_protocol.md` section 6: a runner that
regenerates the fixture, executes both arms, and writes evidence CSVs; a
checker that reruns into scratch space, diffs the recorded CSVs, and guards
package scope (`R`, `src`, `tests`, `NAMESPACE`, `DESCRIPTION`, `man`,
`inst/design`); and an inventory showing one gutted path failing and listing
what was demoted, deleted, and learned. Identity uses `config_hash`, the
snapshot hash, and run-store identity, or is labelled `proto:` once. No hash
ledgers, registries, first-appearance records, ancestry or clean-workspace
gates, file-type tripwires, or review modes. The harness stays within 1,500
lines; a correction diff over 500 lines is a stop-and-talk.

## Measurement boundary

Same host, same R and dependency versions for both arms, one fresh process per
run with its own scratch store and run ID, one unmeasured warm-up and three
measured runs per arm, medians beside raw values. Wall is `proc.time()` around
`ledgr_run()` and ledgr's `t_loop` (`R/fold-engine.R:1113-1141`, spanning the
transaction, the loop, the final bind, and persistence). Process peak working
set comes from an external per-process sampler. Profiler samples, positive
Vcell growth, `object.size()` of retained objects, and process working set are
four distinct quantities, never substituted for one another. GREEN requires
the alternative's median `t_loop` and peak working set both below the current
path's medians by more than the current path's own run-to-run spread at the
40-pulse shape, after every evidence claim holds. Structural numbers from this
fixture never become a public ranking or product promise.

Terminal outcomes, as interpretations of one result rather than gates: GREEN,
the alternative preserves the bounded claims and removes the diagnosed
mechanism with materially lower same-host wall and peak memory; RED, it cannot
preserve the binding evidence contract or does not remove the mechanism;
INCONCLUSIVE, the measurement cannot separate the arms within this boundary.

## Non-goals

Provider, membership, status, lifetime, or valuation optimization;
`execution_view()` redesign; evidence retention tiers or dropping, sampling,
or coalescing diagnostic rows; schema, hash, snapshot, experiment, or public
API redesign; a second or compiled execution engine; parallel pulse execution;
Sharadar-specific code or licensed fixtures; broad package-wide collapse
adoption; choosing a release number; public benchmark or performance claims.
