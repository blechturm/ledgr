## Decision

The maintainer accepts the response's framing. Seed v2 is written on its
terms:

- no descriptor-driven preparation or read dispatch; the guard is a written
  constraint in the interface table: later contracted data may extend
  preparation, P1's cutoff-bound read service and the P2 adapter, never
  through a second causal reader or execution path (response finding 1);
- no third runtime clock: effective and knowledge time plus a read policy on
  a declared observation axis, with delayed first filings stated; vendor
  acquisition time stays with the later data RFC (finding 2);
- the owner assessment covers production, scheduling, application,
  publication, reconstruction, identity and failure ordering, by intended
  use; observable data is not assumed to stay out of P3 to P5 (finding 3);
- no new absence semantics now; later typed absence evidence sits beside
  prepared values (finding 4);
- a fifth scenario, "new observable data kinds", and a sparse dated events
  scenario, installed at acceptance through addressed amendments to D1,
  skeleton Section 2.1 and the extension synthesis's four-scenario reference
  (findings 5 and 7);
- the evidence claims are qualified as the response lists (finding 5); the
  probe commit `fa49d62` exists in ledgr-research ("SF1 point-in-time probe:
  one-page findings"), checked by Claude on 2026-11-04;
- the partial gate stays as already installed in macro WS16 and is cited as
  active, not proposed (finding 7).
