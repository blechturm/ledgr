The separate A0 invocation is assigned to the reviewer, but LDG-3135 and LDG-3134 record it as ticket acceptance evidence.** `:351-352` says "The reviewer separately invokes A0's check.R whole"; `:371` has the implementer record reproduction commands; acceptance `:379` requires "A0's separate pinned/scratch invocation passes with a recorded pointer"; and LDG-3134 `:325-327` and `:335` lean on that same invocation for the three references' "independent-check evidence". Separation is a call-tree property (`:352`, `:362`), not a question of who types the command.
