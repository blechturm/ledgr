# Closeout: Snapshot Hash Rule-3 Conformance Spike

**Outcome:** GREEN under charter Section 4: the exact rule-3 candidate produced
portable deterministic roots, met both cost gates and stayed block-bounded.

## Question Answered

Seed v2's R-owned, column-major binary candidate can define one portable
snapshot root at the shapes v0.2.2 must support. This is evidence for the RFC
synthesis. It does not bind the design or implement production hashing.

## Executed Evidence

The runner first emitted a one-bar root before any expected root or checker
existed. It then froze ten cases generated by observed requirements:

- dense baseline and insertion/fetch/thread invariance;
- the 10,000-row block boundary;
- mixed-case and non-ASCII identifiers;
- two timestamp labels for the same DST instant;
- rounding, large-number, negative-zero and missing-value edges;
- all current fact families and the exact quarantine projection;
- an ignored quarantine column;
- one covered-value mutation; and
- hostile input order.

Every deterministic CSV row is identical on Windows and Ubuntu. The mutation
moves the root; the hostile order refuses. The checker reruns both systems,
diffs the deterministic evidence and guards package scope.

## Cost and Memory

The recorded encoder-only median at five million bars was 0.676 microseconds
per bar on Windows and 0.999 on Ubuntu, below the one-microsecond limit.
Endpoint exponents across 0.5 to 5 million rows were 1.038 and 1.001, below
1.10. The checker rerun measured 0.682/0.990 and 1.041/0.996 respectively.

The streaming five-million-bar worker peaked at 149 MB on Windows and 130 MB
on Ubuntu. It retained no more than the fixed 10,000-row logical block in R.
The separately reported 300,000-row fact projection produced 65.7 MB of JSON
and peaked at 418/474 MB. The fact peak is a residual implementation cost,
not evidence for weakening the fact identity.

## Gut and Containment

The gut disables the byte-order assertion. The hostile case becomes accepted
and deterministic evidence changes, so the checker detects it. The harness is
1,372 lines of R, below its 1,500-line budget. No production code, tests,
contracts, tickets, schema, marker, public API or private data was touched.

## RFC Consumption

The synthesis may now bind the exact Section 5 bytes and cut the fast-hash
ticket before the process-local reuse ticket. The ticket evidence should keep
the absolute cost and growth gates and disclose the retained fact-projection
memory. Product integration, old-rule refusal, complete consumer coverage and
verified-set invalidation remain implementation work.
