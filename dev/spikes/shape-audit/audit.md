# Non-base-R shapes in hot loops: audit of release/v0.2.3 @ 8f4cffc8

Rule audited: optimization_coding_style.qmd, Rules, "Base-R representations inside the loop". JSON sites are excluded (see dev/spikes/json-hot-path-audit/, rows H1-H13).

Method. Counts come from `trace()` against the installed copy of 145cc93b; the fold code is unchanged since. The fixture has 20 instruments x 60 pulses. The strategy trades instruments 1-7 every pulse, giving 413 fills (7 x 59). That keeps per-fill (413), per-pulse (60) and per-instrument-pulse (1200) counts distinct. It ran on both producers, through `ledgr_run`, `ledgr_sweep` (canonical R), readers, `print` and `summary`. Two traces were made:
- `fn_count.R`: every ledgr namespace function, counted per phase (`fn_counts_wide.csv`).
- `base_count2.R`: base, stats, tibble and collapse primitives, attributed to their nearest ledgr caller or handler closure (`base_counts2.csv`).

Profile shares come from dev/spikes/pmwr-performance/profile_*_total.csv (500 x 1260, total.pct). Workloads are dense unless the profile is f_s_avail:
- memory: canonical sweep
- fifo: compiled sweep
- durable: run
- floor: no fill
- f_s_dense / f_s_avail: no-fill runs that carry state

Read them as shares, not seconds.

The scripts hard-code the scratch library (ledgr 145cc93b installed from `git archive`) and an output folder in their first lines; edit those to rerun. The 4.9 GB copy figure for `ledgr_fill_row_buffer_add_many()` (P18) is in the WS4a carrier probe findings on branch `codex/ws4a-carrier-probe`, not yet merged.

## Boundary sites that are correct under the rule

- The `ctx` list with `class = "ledgr_pulse_context"`, built once per pulse (fold-engine.R:756-777); `ctx$hold()`, `ctx$flat()` and `ctx$tradable()` return named vectors (pulse-context.R:1111-1153); `ledgr_history_view` is a lazy cutoff view that records only the column (history-frame.R:484).
- The first `ledgr_validate_strategy_targets` call (fold-engine.R:872), which normalizes the return once.
- Persistence frames manifested once per flush or chunk:
  - `flush_pending` and `flush_states` (output-handler.R:148-161, 672-695);
  - the diagnostic writer's `build` at drain or chunk (availability-diagnostic-writer.R:128-141); its per-pulse writes are typed `setv` by index, which is the reference pattern;
  - the finalize equity frame;
  - `inline_summary` and `materialize_events`, once per candidate.
- Public reader tibbles (`ledgr_accounting_fills_from_replay`, chunked `ledgr_extract_fills_impl`), `print` formatting, and `ledgr_run_equity_prefix_merge` (`rbind`, once per run).

- Walk-forward and run_window folds were not traced.
- Ownership is uncertain for P4 (no named owner) and P15 (whether "diagnostic flushing" covers the commit reconciliation is the same question as H5).

## Summary

- 40 hot rows: 11 per fill, 4 per instrument-pulse, 18 per pulse, 7 per event row.
- Rows per shape (a row can carry several): (1) data frames 5; (2) per-element lists 18; (3) S3/class dispatch 8; (4) POSIXct 9; (5) name matching 18; (6) growing or copy-on-append 2; (7) R loops 13.
- Owner "none": 6 rows (F7, I1, I3, P4, P17, P18). F10 is partly unowned (the envelope).
- The three most costly:
  1. **F7, lot accounting per fill.** Axis-wide name lookups plus copy-on-modify of instrument-length lot state. 19% of a canonical sweep and 28% of a durable run, growing with universe size. No owner in the macro text.
  2. **F1 + F2, per-fill plan objects and timestamp revalidation.** The plan is 25-28% of a sweep; timestamps are 11-13%. Owned by WS4a.
  3. **I1 + P18 + F10, the compiled path.** Lot pack and unpack loop over every instrument each pulse with fills (18%), and `add_many` copies whole columns (9%). About 27% of a compiled sweep with no owner.
- Runner-up: P9, the eager `ctx$vec` / `bar_index` name matching in both modes, about 19% of the dense no-fill floor. Only WS5 covers it on dense.
