# JSON on hot paths: audit of release/v0.2.3 @ 01ac82fd

Measured with call counts, not timings: `trace()` on `canonical_json`, `ledgr_canonical_json_encode`, `.ledgr_json_cache_key`, `ledgr_json_read_nested`, `ledgr_json_read_config` and `digest::digest`, run against the installed HEAD 145cc93b. The fold code is the same. The fixture was 20 instruments x 60 pulses with 1180 fills, an alternating buy-10/flat strategy, and both availability and dense producers, through `ledgr_run`, `ledgr_sweep` and the readers. Scripts and counts are in this folder: `json_count*.R` and `counts*.csv`.

The scripts hard-code the scratch library (ledgr 145cc93b installed from `git archive`) and an output folder in lines 1-4; edit those to rerun.

## Could not classify with confidence

- H7, H8 and H11 were classified by reading, not execution: the fixture had no corporate-action, disposition or opening facts. Their frequency is bounded by the number of such events, not by pulses.
- H3's ownership is split between the WS5 amendment (typed stored form) and the WS4b amendment (padding and serialization in the floor). Neither says who removes the `canonical_json` cache-key hash (H4) or the sweep's discarded encode. That falls out only if both land.
- H5: "diagnostic flushing" in the WS4b amendment may or may not cover the reconciliation `detail_json` encode. Needs a maintainer reading.
- The trace ran against the installed copy of 145cc93b, not 01ac82fd. The commits since then touch only design and spike files, so the fold code is the same.

## Summary

- 13 hot rows (H1-H13). Owner "none": **H6** (per-pulse `intent_reason` encode, any mode) and **H13** (its reader). H4 (the cache-key hash) has no owner of its own and only disappears once H3/H5/H6 do.
- The profile's `.ledgr_json_cache_key`/`digest::digest` per-pulse floor comes from two `canonical_json` calls per availability pulse: `fold-engine.R:1521` (padded carried state, encoded even for stateless strategies) and `:1462` (reconciliation detail). `canonical_json` hashes the whole serialized object each time to look up its cache. For an unchanged padded state that hash is the whole cost.
- A sweep encodes strategy state every pulse and then discards it (memory sink no-op).
- Readers re-parse every fill's JSON per call: fills, trades and `summary()` each did 1180/1180.
- Not owned beyond its JSON: the quadratic per-pulse full replay in terminal recovery (H10c).
