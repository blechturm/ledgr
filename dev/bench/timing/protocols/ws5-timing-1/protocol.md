# Protocol ws5-timing-1

This is the descriptive Cut 7 timing preregistration owned by LDG-3179. It
uses the unchanged shared paired harness, eight alternating pairs per cell and
the frozen WS4a economic fixtures. The benchmark-only context adapter in
`workloads.R` expresses the same strategy against the old and new public
surfaces; it is not product compatibility code.

The six named cells cross fixed-basket and declared-session clocks with
no-fill, sparse-rebalance and wide stateful work. Two further fixed-basket wide
cells hold the economics constant while state changes or remains unchanged.
The construction cell times `ledgr_experiment()` separately before any claim
about a warm fold iteration.

Run two labels and keep their records separate:

- `14252ffb` versus the integrated Cut 7 candidate is the macro-rule-16
  release comparison;
- the post-WS4a attribution base `b5622f09` versus that candidate locates the costs added
  after the typed carrier.

For both state cells, the milestone record must report fold-stage `t_ctx` and
`t_state` shares from the existing phase-attribution telemetry beside the
shared-harness clock. No new timer is inserted in the fold. The economic
controls are status, closed trades and final equity, as in WS4a; every repeat
must equal its warm-up outcome.

The 1.10 ratios are disposition triggers, not semantic acceptance gates. A
failed or slower cell is explained and routed under macro rule 16; no oracle,
fixture, pair count or threshold is changed to hide it.
